FFZ · Mesa, Arizona

Launch Mesa at Falcon Field

Groundbreaking Nov–Dec 2026 Delivery planned Sept 2027

Ten executive hangars on the airfield, about 74,000 square feet in all. Eight units include a two-story office and lounge with a rooftop deck over the airport. Two are storage hangars. Every unit has a 66-foot door that fits any midsize jet, plus a roll-up door so you can drive in from the landside.

Falcon Field trained British Royal Air Force pilots during World War II. We think the buildings on it should still look the part.
