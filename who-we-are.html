Who we are

A family company from Provo

We are a family of pilots and aviation enthusiasts. We went looking for a hangar and found steel buildings that cost more than the planes inside them. So we started building our own.

Launch Development Group is owned by its four founders. Bill brings 30 years of running a retail business and the supply chain behind it. Thomas and Andrew handle capital and projects. Charlie is on the job site.

Founders

The Freedmans

BF
Founder & CEO

Bill Freedman

Bill spent more than 30 years building Downeast Outfitters into a furniture and apparel chain with 60+ stores and over 1,000 employees across the Intermountain West. The company was acquired in 2020. In 2009 he and his brothers were named EY Entrepreneur of the Year in retail and consumer products. After the sale he started Launch to take on the hangar shortage.

TF
Co-founder · Finance & capital

Thomas Freedman

Thomas graduated cum laude in finance from BYU and is a Moelis Fellow. He has worked in lower-middle-market private equity and real estate development, and most recently as an investor at Partners Group. At Launch he works with lenders and investors.

AF
Co-founder · Projects

Andrew Freedman

Andrew studied finance at BYU and has worked in corporate strategy and portfolio management, where he helped manage more than $100 million in funds. He helped develop the Freedman Hangar at Provo and leads our work with airports and cities.

CF
Co-founder · Construction

Charlie Freedman

Charlie studies construction and facilities management at BYU. He worked on the Provo hangar from the ground up and acts as owner's representative on our job sites.
