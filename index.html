The building

More than a steel box with a big door

Most hangars in the U.S. went up during the 1980s and '90s, and it shows. We wanted one that works better and looks better than corrugated steel, at a price close to a conventional hangar. Bill Freedman's 30 years of supplier relationships in retail are how we keep it there.
