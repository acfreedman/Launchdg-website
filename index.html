The building

More than a steel box with a big door

Most hangars in the U.S. went up during the 1980s and '90s, and it shows. We started Launch because we wanted a hangar that worked better and looked better than corrugated steel, at a price close to what a conventional hangar costs.

We source buildings, doors and components directly through supplier relationships Bill Freedman built over 30 years in retail. That is how we keep the price down without cutting what goes into the building.

  1. Insulated metal panels and parapet roofs
  2. Rooftop patio and observation deck, sized for a grill and a hot tub
  3. Roll-up garage door with landside access for your car
  4. Extensive glass on the lounge and office side
  5. Executive restroom in every unit
  6. Higher Power hydraulic hangar doors for clearance and reliability
For airports

We bring the capital

Airports have land and long waitlists, but hangar construction is usually left to whoever is willing to pay for it. We finance our projects privately, pay ground rent from the day the lease is signed, and build to FAA design standards and the airport's minimum standards.
